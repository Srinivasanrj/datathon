FIR Number: FIR-2025-BEL-015
Division: Belagavi Dist
Date: 2025-07-08
Crime Category: Arms Act
Narrative: Intelligence-led raid resulted in the seizure of 24 country-made pistols. The weapons were being manufactured in illegal foundries that were disguised externally as agricultural tool sheds. Suspects used standard lathe machines to bore barrels, supplying local gangs across the border.
