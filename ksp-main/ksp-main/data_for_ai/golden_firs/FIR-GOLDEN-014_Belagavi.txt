FIR Number: FIR-2025-BEL-014
Division: Belagavi Dist
Date: 2025-11-15
Crime Category: Human Trafficking / Fraud
Narrative: A fake nursing job recruitment agency busted. Suspects targeted unemployed rural youth with false promises of high-paying hospital jobs abroad. The entire operation was run out of a remote rented farmhouse near the Maharashtra border, acting as a transit point before moving victims.
