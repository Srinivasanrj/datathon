FIR Number: FIR-2025-TUM-012
Division: Tumakuru
Date: 2025-12-05
Crime Category: Agricultural Theft
Narrative: Reports of organized theft targeting rural farms. Suspects are stealing high-value arecanut yields and stripping copper irrigation pipes from water pumps. Multiple eyewitnesses reported seeing the suspects loading the stolen goods into a white Bolero pickup truck with a distinctly faded red front bumper.
