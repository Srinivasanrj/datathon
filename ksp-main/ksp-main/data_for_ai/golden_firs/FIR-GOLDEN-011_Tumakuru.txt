FIR Number: FIR-2025-TUM-011
Division: Tumakuru
Date: 2025-10-30
Crime Category: Highway Dacoity
Narrative: Organized highway robbery on NH48. Suspects hide in the median bushes at night. Their M.O. involves throwing raw eggs at the windshields of passing cars. When drivers use wipers, it smears the glass, forcing them to stop. Suspects then surround the vehicle and rob the occupants at knifepoint.
